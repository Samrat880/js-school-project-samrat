Car Industry Timeline

Date & Event Description

The first full-scale working steam-powered vehicle is built by Nicolas-Joseph Cugnot.

Karl Benz patents the Motorwagen, considered the first true automobile.

The Ford Model T is introduced, revolutionizing mass production in the automotive industry.

The first all-steel body car, the Chevrolet Series AA Capitol, is produced.

The golden age of American automobiles with tailfins and chrome detailing.

The oil crisis leads to a shift towards smaller, more fuel-efficient cars.

Toyota introduces the Prius, the first mass-produced hybrid car.

AMG is established as a racing engine forge, marking the beginning of a legendary partnership in high-performance vehicles with Mercedes-Benz.

The rise of autonomous vehicles and increased focus on sustainability in car manufacturing.
